The story
ai = Σj G mj (rj − ri) / |rj − ri|³

In 1687 Isaac Newton's Principia solved two bodies exactly: a planet and the Sun trace a perfect ellipse, forever. Add a third and the problem has defeated mathematicians ever since. In 1885 King Oscar II of Sweden announced a prize for a solution. Henri Poincaré won it, then found a mistake in his own winning paper. Fixing it, he discovered that some orbits are tangled beyond any simple formula: the first glimpse of chaos. Order is rare: the figure-eight, found by computer in 1993 and proved in 2000, and the new families discovered in 2013 are special starting points where three bodies chase each other forever.
