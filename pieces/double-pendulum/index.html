The story

A double pendulum follows simple, exact physics, with no randomness at all. Yet if two start a billionth of a degree apart, they soon do completely different things. That sensitivity is called chaos, and it's why weather can't be forecast more than about two weeks ahead. The colours show each pendulum's starting position: when the rainbow appears, you're watching invisible differences become visible.
